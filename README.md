## About me

I am a **Lecturer and Researcher in Cybersecurity at Hanze University of Applied Sciences** in Groningen, the Netherlands. My work focuses on the intersection of **machine learning, digital forensics, cybersecurity, and large language models**, with an emphasis on evidence-centric and reproducible investigation workflows.

My PhD research examined **AI-driven anomaly detection for web service attacks** using machine-learning and text-mining techniques. Previously, I served as a **Lecturer in Cybersecurity at UNSW Sydney**. I am also an **IEEE Senior Member** and **Certified EC-Council Instructor (CEI)**.

## Research interests

`Machine Learning` · `AI in Digital Forensics` · `Cybersecurity` · `LLMs in Cybersecurity` · `Anomaly Detection` · `Evidence-Centric Digital Investigation`

## Featured research

### Hybrid ML–Graph–LLM Forensics
A grounded hybrid framework for evidence-centric Windows digital forensic investigation that combines machine learning, deterministic evidence graphs, and grounded LLM reasoning.

**Focus:** Digital Forensics · Machine Learning · Evidence Graphs · Grounded LLMs · Explainable AI

🔒 **Private research repository — access by invitation**

### Text Mining-Based Anomaly Detection
Research on text-mining and machine-learning methods for detecting anomalous behaviour in web services, network traffic, and security data.

**Focus:** Text Mining · Anomaly Detection · Machine Learning · Cybersecurity
